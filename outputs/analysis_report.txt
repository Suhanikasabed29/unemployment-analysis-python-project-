7. POLICY-RELEVANT INSIGHTS
- Monitor unemployment at both national and regional levels because regional averages differ substantially.
- During sudden shocks, monthly labour-market monitoring can help identify rapid changes and support timely employment interventions.
- Rural and urban labour markets should be analyzed separately where the data supports that distinction.
- Seasonal variation can be considered when interpreting monthly unemployment movements.
- The results should be combined with wage, sector, inflation, migration and labour-force data before making policy decisions.

8. LIMITATIONS
- The datasets cover a limited time window and therefore cannot represent long-run historical unemployment.
- The COVID comparison is observational and descriptive, not a causal impact study.
- Regional coverage differs between the supplied files.
- Average rates can hide important differences between states, cities, sectors and demographic groups.
